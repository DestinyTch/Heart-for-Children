Our Mission

No Child Should
Go to Sleep Hungry.

Hearts for Children operates a direct-to-child model—no bloated overhead, no middlemen. Every dollar is tracked on our public ledger and converted into real aid within 72 hours of receipt.

We accept crypto, gift cards, and traditional currency because generosity shouldn't be gatekept by banking infrastructure. Your Steam balance can feed a child in Nairobi tonight.
